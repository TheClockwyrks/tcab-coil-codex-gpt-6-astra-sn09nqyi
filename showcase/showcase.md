# Coil

Find your line. Feed the chain. **Coil** is a neon arcade serpent: a mint-green streak on a dark circuit grid, chasing the next flash of amber. Every pellet makes you longer. Every turn takes a little more room away.

Steer with the arrow keys or WASD. You can turn sideways, but never double back. Keep your head clear of the walls and your own trail; one collision ends the run. The pace stays steady, so the challenge is reading the space ahead.

Survival is only half the game. Reach the next pellet before your combo window closes and your multiplier climbs, up to x5. Take the short route, watch the amber bar, and decide how much space you can afford to trade for points.

A crisp bite, a spray of sparks, and a rising electronic chime mark each successful chain. Pause whenever you need a breath, then return to exactly the line you left. Your session best is waiting to be beaten.
